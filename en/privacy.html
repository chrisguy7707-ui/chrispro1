Privacy policy

Your photos and tech packs stay in your browser.

Jakji (the “Site”) values your privacy and complies with Korea's Personal Information Protection Act and related laws. This policy explains what information the Site handles and how. The Korean version is the official one; if they differ, the Korean policy prevails.

Feedback

‘Send feedback’ collects the message you write, an email address if you want a reply (optional), and — only if you agree — device info (browser, screen size, current view and chosen item). Garment photos and work content are never sent.

Feedback is delivered to the operator's mailbox through the form service Formspree (privacy policy) and may be kept on its servers for a period under its policy. We use feedback only to improve the service and reply, and delete reply email addresses on our side within one year of handling. Send deletion requests via Contact.

My styles and share links

Work saved in ‘My styles’ (including photos) is kept only in your browser's storage (IndexedDB) and never sent to our server. Clearing browser data deletes it.

A ‘Share link’ compresses the tech pack (without the photo; cost and margin only if you choose) into the part of the link after #. That part is not sent to our server, but anyone who has the link can see its contents, so check who you send it to.

1. Information we collect

The Site has no accounts and does not directly collect or store personal information such as names or contact details on a server.

  • Tech pack content and garment photos: processed only in your browser and never sent to our server. They remain only in files you save yourself and in your browser's storage.
  • Access logs: our host (GitHub Pages) may log technical information such as IP addresses for security and operations, under its own policy.
  • Visit statistics: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how often pages are viewed. It uses no cookies, doesn't identify individual visitors or follow them across sites, and collects only summary data such as page address, referrer, country, browser/device type and load speed. Tech pack content and photos are not included. To count only that main buttons such as ‘Print’, ‘Save’, ‘Export’, ‘Copy share link’ and the template download were pressed, the tool loads an empty page (for example /e/print.html) invisibly once when you press the button, and it is counted as a page view. It carries no information about which work, photos, text you typed or your device. Nothing is counted when you view a shared tech pack or when your browser's Do Not Track setting is on. See the Cloudflare privacy policy; you can block it with your browser's tracking protection or an extension.

2. Photo recognition

Default (on-device): when you upload a photo, a recognition model runs inside your browser to pick the item, details and color. The photo is not sent off your device. The first time, the program and model files are downloaded from jsDelivr and Hugging Face, which may receive connection information such as your IP address. Downloaded files are cached in your browser and can be cleared in its settings.

Claude (only when opened inside claude.ai): the photo is sent to Anthropic through your claude.ai account and handled under Anthropic's policy. Otherwise photos never leave your device.

3. Browser storage

The following is stored only in your browser's local storage (localStorage) and cache, never on our server. Clear site data in your browser settings to remove it.

  • Autosave: the tech pack in progress, edited measurements, choices, reference photo and pattern inputs (for ‘Continue’ next time)
  • Recognition model files and download record: so they aren't downloaded again
  • Language choice: whether you chose Korean or English
  • Display mode: whether you chose auto, light or dark
  • App files (cache): for “Install app” and offline use, the site's page files (HTML, scripts, styles, icons) are kept in the browser cache. Photos and your work are not included.
  • Favorites: the names of sub-categories and fabrics, and the IDs of “My styles”, that you added to the Favorites tab (no work content)

Files downloaded with ‘Save file’ (.json) are stored on your device and include the reference photo, so be careful when sharing them.

4. Cookies and ads (Google AdSense)

The Site may show Google AdSense ads to cover running costs.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendors' cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • See How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

You can refuse cookies in your browser settings; some ads may then be shown as non-personalized ads.

5. External services

The Site loads fonts and programs from jsDelivr and model files from Hugging Face. These services may receive information such as your IP address.

6. Your rights

The Site keeps no information on its servers that identifies you, so there is no separate data to view or delete. Information in your browser can be deleted at any time in its settings. Send privacy questions via Contact.

7. Children under 14

The Site does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the update on this page with its effective date.
