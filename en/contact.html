When reporting a bug

Including the following helps us check quickly:

  • Device and browser (e.g. iPhone Safari, Windows Chrome)
  • The item, gender, size and fit you chose
  • Which button you pressed and what went wrong
Before sending garment photos or tech packs, make sure they're OK to share. Posts on the GitHub issue board are public.
